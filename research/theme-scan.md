primary (the colour that fills the main action) and secondary (the highlight colour), effects,
and radius/spacing notes. Totals and the derived rules are at the end.

## Derived rules for the three themes

1. **Light and dark are neutral, not brand.** Primary actions are ink in light and white in dark.
   The user's colours never tint the neutrals in these two themes.
2. **Brand theme = the board's "brand canvas" mode.** The canvas is the user's *primary* colour.
   Surfaces are clear glass tinted by it (white at 14%), cards that need contrast are white. Text and
   the primary action are white on dark primaries and ink on light primaries (chosen by measured
   contrast, never by hue name). The user's *secondary* colour is the highlight: active segment,
   selected datum, tags, check marks.
3. **The secondary colour is a highlight in all three themes.** Light and dark use it for the
   same 1–3 accent spots; the brand theme does too. That makes the same screen work in every
   theme without code changes.
4. **Status colours stay fixed** (success green, warning amber, danger red, info blue), each with
   a light and dark tone, so meaning never changes with the brand.
5. **Radius, spacing, type and controls don't change per theme.** Only colour, material and shadow
   do. (Confirmed: cards 24–32, orbs 40–56, pills 32–56 across all three modes.)
